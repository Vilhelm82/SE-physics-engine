## The lineage strike — read this before claiming anything

Will searched, same session, and found:

- **Chien–Waldron**, arXiv:1312.5393 (SIAM JDM 2016) — projective unitary
  equivalence of finite frames via Bargmann products on a **cycle-space
  basis**, including the anchored four-cycle decomposition. This is the
  $\beta_1$/graph-cohomology side.
- **Li–Wagner–Zhang**, arXiv:2507.14878, **Thm V.1** — *every* $n$-th order
  single-qubit Bargmann invariant satisfies a monic quadratic over the
  two-state-overlap field, determined up to one global conjugation. Proved by
  induction for **all $n$** and for **mixed** states. Their proof's property
  (A) is exactly the Binet–Cauchy engine.
- **Zhang–Xie–Li**, PRA 111, 042417 (2025) — the explicit $n=3,4,5$ algebra;
  their $n=3$ *is* our closed form.

**Verified correspondence** (exact, `sympy`): with $B=A+iV_0D$, $V_0^2=\Delta$,
their $P_3 = A$ and $Q_3 = A^2+\Delta D^2$, and our $B$ satisfies their
quadratic identically. Two languages, one theorem.

**Novelty withdrawn.** The graph descent theorem is **[retrodiction]** as
mathematics. What remains ours: the *instantiation* — $\arg B$ as the
orientation-sheet meter of this cover, tied to the Cayley $\Delta$ and the
click-equivariant Kummer module — and the arithmetic reading (all cycles
inhabit one shared orientation extension while local presentations inhabit a
much larger cover), which LWZ do not foreground.
