## What the numbers say, tiered

**[proved | one line, already in the spec]** F^dag F = I - K^dag K exactly (checked to 1e-11 on every case), so the
stacked leak is a scaled isometry exactly when the survivor's success is state-independent. The accumulator
channel {G J, G H} with J^2 + H^2 = I is a dephasing between the two eigenvectors of F^dag F with off-diagonal
factor sqrt((1-p1)(1-p2)) + sqrt(p1 p2); expanding gives 1 - F_avg = conditional + c delta^2/6 + O(c^2 delta^2).

**[numerical]** The measured self-calibrated error / floor ratio is 1.19, 1.19, 1.19 (trine) and 1.32, 1.32, 1.31
(five) across the three gains -- constant, so the O(c^2) and conditional terms are the whole discrepancy and
the floor formula is the leading term.

**[numerical]** DB_stack scales as eps^3 on both words: x7.9 and x7.8 per doubling (trine), x7.9 and x7.8 (five).
Therefore the accumulator's unconditional gain error c delta^2/6 scales as eps^2 * eps^6 = **eps^8**: measured
ratios 248 and 238 (trine), 247 and 236 (five), against 2^8 = 256. The unconditional error is now the same
order in gain as the survivor's conditional error (FD-3's eighth order), with no post-selection.

**[numerical, correction to the runner]** The runner's printed "exponent 5.94" fits raw excess against two
asymmetric points and folds in the growth of c(eps). Normalising: (excess/c) at 2% over (excess/c) at 0.5% is
3.99 (trine) and 4.01 (five) = ((0.01)/(0.005))^2. So the fixed-calibration excess is A * c(eps) * (eps - eps_0)^2
with A = 13.2 (trine), 35.9 (five). Exponent 2 in the mismatch, as predicted; prefactor ~10-40 c, larger than
the "order c" guessed. Calibration mismatch is the dominant cost off the calibration point; a rate-tracked
U_cal (from the register click rate, data-blind at aggregate level) is the obvious next control.

**[numerical]** Five-loop word is tight in aggregate: DB_stack 4.2e-6 at 1% gain. The live FD gate already
has the property the accumulator needs. It is also far blinder to detuning leak than the trine (DB 0.042 vs 0.68).

**[numerical]** Detuning passes through as expected: at (0.01, 1e-4, 0) the trine accumulator removes the whole
gain erasure and leaves 2.59e-8 against a conditional 2.43e-8 (ratio 1.07). On five the mixed case is set by
the detuning-induced tight-frame deviation (DB 1.9e-3 -> floor 1.56e-9 = measured 1.56e-9). Detuning is
NOT recovered by the accumulator, as the spec said; it is neither helped nor harmed beyond that.

**[fork-logic note]** For detuning-only cases the runner labels fork (a) because self/floor <= 10, but there
discard ~ conditional (c ~ 1e-9), so forks (a) and (b) coincide and the accumulator is idle. Recorded, not rerun.
