# RH-4 -- the register at the absorbing interface: what it must be, in FD's own units

**[numerical | FD's instrument unchanged except the sink; 14-dim density-matrix propagation; decoder = Codex's stack]**
A coherent capture placed BEFORE each of FD's absorbing holds, into a fresh pair of register modes per dump, reproduces
FD exactly when switched off (herald and Pauli vector to 1e-16), takes precisely what FD absorbs when switched on
(captured fraction 2.650547e-3 vs FD herald 2.650548e-3), and at ideal register gives herald 2e-16 with the unheralded
rate at FD's background floor 4.44e-6. Decoder (d = 3 X+Z / d = 5 X+Z, 200k shots each): **FD 445 / 71; ideal capture 341 / 50**.
The register beats FD with margin if its loss and dephasing rates are <= 1e-3 per unit time (T_1, T_2 >= ~8 word durations),
even with UNMONITORED loss and a 5% capture-angle error; it ties FD at ~1e-2 (register lifetime below one word) when the
loss is monitored or the error is dephasing; it loses to FD only when loss at ~1e-2 is unmonitored.

**[provenance]** `rlq/rh4_capture.py` (Codex's venv), receipt `rh4_capture-checks.json`, log `rh4_capture.log`, 6/6 checks +
8 decoder scenarios x 4 codes. Operating point n = 1, gain 1%, Delta_d/a = 1e-4, gamma/a = 1e-6, kappa/a = 10, kappa t_d = 40;
capture pulse duration t_c = 1/a per hold (T = 129.87 vs FD's 124.87). Written and run 2026-09-06 by Claire.

## The model, declared
- Space P + Q + R_1..R_5 (14 dims, single excitation). Loops: `loop_endpoints_with_effects` (ramps, inverses, stretches,
  detuning, background gamma inside the no-jump propagators, exact lost-effect operators). Holds: FD's absorbing
  diag(e^{-20}, e^{-20}, e^{-i delta_d t_d}, e^{-i delta_r t_d}), monitored -> herald. UNCHANGED.
- Added: before each absorbing hold, H_cap = g sum_m (|m><r_km| + h.c.) for t_c, angle g t_c = (pi/2)(1 + e_c); background on Q
  and detuning phases on P during t_c. What the capture misses is absorbed and heralded by FD's dump: the fallback is FD.
- Register decoherence from fill time to readout: loss gamma_r (heralded if monitored, else I/2) and dephasing gamma_phi
  (coherences between distinct register modes decay at gamma_phi per filled mode).
- Readout: projective 'register occupied'; register branch -> U_cal^dag (10 -> 2) then G, U_cal from the ideal-register
  model at the operating point (self-calibrated; e_c and decoherence unknown to it); leftover heralded; residual Q -> I/2.
- Channel: exact Pauli twirl of the accepted map from its Pauli transfer matrix; herald = the I/2-input value (the twirl
  folds the small state-dependence, spread ~1e-6, into the average), qec_distance_stack's format.

## Reading, tiered

**[numerical]** Three degradation channels, three behaviours:
- Capture-angle error is FREE: what the pulse misses, FD's absorber heralds. e_c = 5% costs 1.6e-5 herald and nothing else.
- Register LOSS is either a herald (monitored) or an unlocated error (unmonitored). Monitored, it never loses to FD at any
  rate tried -- at gamma_r = 1e-2 (T_1 below one word) it still recovers 58% of the captured shots and ties FD. Unmonitored,
  it crosses FD between 1e-3 and 1e-2, i.e. r_eff between 0.06 and 0.41, bracketing RH-3's r* in 0.1..0.3.
- Register DEPHASING mostly turns recovery back into heralding: at gamma_phi = 1e-3, 8% of the captured shots leave the
  recovery isometry's range and are heralded as leftover, 2% become unlocated; at 1e-2 the split is 48% / 12% and the decoder
  ties FD. Dephasing is the gentlest failure mode because the recovery isometry projects the dephased part out.

**[derived]** The requirement, in FD's units (a = 1, word ~ 125-130): a 2N = 10-mode coherent register with loss and dephasing
rates <= 1e-3, i.e. T_1, T_2 >= ~10 word durations, plus an occupancy readout and a 10 -> 2 mode recombination whose own
errors count as register dephasing. Meet that and the gate is invisible to the decoder; miss it by an order of magnitude and
you have FD back, provided the register's failures are monitored.

**[not modelled]** the occupancy readout and the 10 -> 2 recombination as physical operations (folded into gamma_phi);
register modes with their own detuning; capture pulses of finite bandwidth; anything about the native entangler or hardware;
d = 7, 9 and the bicycle code at this interface; single seeds; RH-3's r* itself is +-0.1.
