## The conjecture

that emits rather than absorbs. What we call the cosmological far field is the Hawking face seen from
the only side that cannot look back through it. Which sheet we are on is $\operatorname{sgn} D$.

## Why it has to be the white sheet, not the black one

The standing objection to any black-hole cosmology is that a Schwarzschild interior *contracts*. Every
version since Pathria (1972) needs something bolted on — torsion, a bounce — to turn the fall into an
expansion. The model does not need the bolt-on: the *other sheet* is the plain time-reverse, and its
interior leaves. The Big Bang is the past singularity; the open circuit before the load closed; the
expansion is the discharge. Dark energy is the far pole of $G$ doing what its name says, on the sheet
where escape is the default.

The usual objection to white holes — that they are unstable, destroyed by blueshifted infall — is
written from the *black* sheet looking at the same object. The sheets are inequivalent irreps: one
absorbs and is stable, one emits. That asymmetry is T8a's theorem wearing physics.

**Formation** needs no mechanism. A white hole is not made; it is the other block of a black hole that
formed in the parent. Every black hole has one.

## The customs house (Will, 2026-09-05)

**Only the page's invariants survive.** A seat's invariant is its own line, the thing it cannot see;
everything else it reads is state, and therefore transactable. So each page taxes exactly what it can
see and spares exactly what it cannot. Cross to the $G$ station and mass is on a visible axis: you lose
mass. Cross to $-c$ and the thermal content is what that page reads: you go cold. The rate per gate is
already derived — transmittance $e^{-\pi\omega/\alpha}$ per crossing, two crossings a Boltzmann factor [T4].

What survives all four crossings is what is invariant on *every* page: the frame's form $q$ (there is one
form for the frame, not one per seat [T7b]) and the deck sign (the loop is what reads it, $W^8 = -1$ on
the cover). **This is the no-hair theorem said from inside**: a hole keeps mass, charge and spin because
those are the frame's invariants and not any page's; the rest is hair because some station can see it.

## Kill conditions

   orders of magnitude ($10^{-30}$ K for the de Sitter horizon, not 2.7). It survives only if the sky
   temperature comes through the *parent's* mass and the seat switch. If the completed model hands back a
   temperature nowhere near 2.7 K, the conjecture dies on the number.
2. **The direction.** Take T7f's interior sector ($|\gamma| > 1$, rulers' span Lorentzian) on the $I = -1$
   block and ask whether the radial direction reads outward. **If both blocks contract, there is no white
   hole in the model and this is over.** This is the same open sign as the horizon sheet and the neutron
   star: $H \prec G$ or $G \prec H$.
3. **Signalling.** The model's non-injective presentations [T7c, P7] are the candidate mechanism for
   correlation without transit. If they also give a usable channel, the model predicts superluminal
   signalling and dies immediately. Runner owed: Bell-violating correlation with no cone-readable ordering.
