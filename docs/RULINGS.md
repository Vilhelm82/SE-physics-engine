# Standing rulings — seated-root

Will's rulings, with the tier each carries and what would overturn it. **A ruling outranks a session's confusion. It does
not outrank a correction that arrives with evidence** — a computation, a measurement, a failed check. If you have
evidence, bring it; if you have a rephrasing, don't.

Last updated 2026-09-07.

## R0. The question that settles every doubt

This model exists to distinguish **what the observer sees** from **what the global model has**. When anything is in
doubt, the question is: **is this invariant, or is it an observer artefact?**

| invariant (frame side, no seat in it) | observer artefact (presentation side) |
|---|---|
| the frame's Gram; det G = 0 as a rank condition | the tilt η |
| sgn D, the block label | the lapse N and everything read through it |
| conditions on the null congruence (θ₊ = 0 where the river runs at c) | the redshift; the pathologies at r_s |
| Ricci-flatness; the Raychaudhuri balance; Damour's coefficients | the sign change inside; the rank loss at η = −1 |

**The presentation is not a distortion of something realer — it is the observed physics.** Every measured constant and
every law lives there (P12). The bare frame is what nobody reads.

*Tier: methodological. Overturned by: a demonstration that some quantity is neither, or that the split is not
well-defined on some object.*

## R1. Seats do not move; the axis they ride does

A seat cannot pivot away from c: a seat **is** a line, c is the unique negative line (T7b2a), and rotors preserve q, so
no isometry takes a seat off its own line. The tilt λ is the rulers' Gram against a **fixed** seat. Write "the frame is
pivoted and the seat reads X", never "the seat pivots".

*Tier: derived (T7b2a) + definitional (P5). Overturned by: an isometry of the frame's form that moves a unit negative
line off itself — i.e. by a failure of T7b2a.*

## R2. The model has no agency

"A model with agency is manufacturing, not observing." No chooser, no settings, no measurements — seats **read**. Any
kill condition containing a verb with no subject in the primitives ("usable", "chosen", "prepared", "measured") is not
a kill condition: testing it requires *adding* an agent, which is the Cl(3) failure one level up.

*Tier: ruling on scope. Overturned by: a dynamics tier in which a state changes along a path, so that two readings on
one path carry an order without anything choosing. Kill 3 of CONJECTURE-COSMOLOGY is withdrawn pending exactly that.*

## R3. Two observers are not two sides of a triangle

Distance is fine. Relative measurement is fine — it is all a seat does. What is forbidden is building a figure out of
two occupants and treating their separation as a side. Multiple observers at different relative tilts all read c as
their constant and **cannot distinguish themselves from each other by it**. Comparison happens at one seat holding two
readings (reunited clocks; a signal arriving at a receiver) — never at a distance between two occupants.

*Tier: derived from P9 + T7c (non-injective presentations). Overturned by: a construction in which a second occupant
enters a reading without being read.*

## R4. Nothing in the frame deforms

The **presentation** warps — G′ = G + η·kkᵀ is a real rank-one difference, and it is the reference frame our physics is
written in. The frame it presents does not strain. Attributing the warp to the frame produced the withdrawn RIDE-2.

*Tier: definitional (the presentation is a map of the frame, not a state of it). Overturned by: a physical quantity
that requires the frame itself to change.*

## R5. The singularity is a seat artefact

Constants are seat-relative; the seat set is a continuum; what the seat is declared to occupy is arbitrary.

*Tier: ruling, supported by T7c's non-injectivity and by the interior sector being a presentation continued past the
last seat that can make it. Overturned by: a seat-independent invariant that is singular at the same place.*

## R6. Analogy stays in chat

Only exact physics labels in files, runners, results pages, ledger entries and commit messages. Analogy may enter
handoffs when **explicitly** framed as a conjecture or framing tool. Analogy language in conversation is welcome and
useful — it just has to be unmistakably marked as analogy.

*Tier: working rule. See AGENTS.md for the two cases that produced it.*
